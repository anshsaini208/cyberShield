What CyberShield does

🔎

Phishing Detection

Detects fake login pages, impersonation patterns, and suspicious brand look-alike domains.

🧾

Credential Protection

Identifies password fields, login forms, and cross-origin submissions that may be stealing credentials.

📊

Evidence & Risk Scoring

Calculates a risk score and explains why a page is dangerous through clear, explainable signals.

How it works

1

Inspect Page

The browser content script checks the active webpage and its form structure.

2

Analyze Signals

It evaluates URL patterns, domain similarity, hidden iframes, redirects, and form actions.

3

Score Threats

The detection engine assigns a risk score and identifies the threat type.

4

Warn User

The popup and evidence panel explain the risk before sensitive data can be entered.

Why this matters

Problem

  • Phishing websites often look nearly identical to trusted sites.
  • Users rely on memory and instinct rather than real-time protection.
  • Credential theft can happen within seconds after a page loads.

Impact

  • Reduces successful phishing attempts before credential entry.
  • Improves user awareness using clear risk explanations.
  • Creates a proactive defense layer for safer browsing.
